what we do

Care that grows with you

Chiropractic

Hands-on assessment and treatment for neck pain, back pain, headaches, sciatica and sports injuries. Spinal adjustments, mobilisation and soft tissue techniques, with a plan built around you.

About chiropractic at Solana Health

Dry needling

Fine, single-use needles placed into tight, tender muscle to release trigger points. Used alongside hands-on treatment when a muscle is slow to let go.

About dry needling

Physiotherapy

Movement-focused rehabilitation after injury or surgery. Physiotherapy is the next service coming to Solana Health, and we'll let you know as soon as bookings open.

meet the team

Angelo Marketos, chiropractor

Angelo has been practising as a chiropractor since 2011, with a particular focus on neck and back pain, headaches and sports injuries. He holds a Bachelor of Chiropractic Science and a Master of Chiropractic, is registered with the Chiropractic Board of Australia and is a member of Chiropractic Australia.
