Getting here

By car: we're on Bunnerong Road in the Matraville shopping strip, between Beauchamp Road and Bay Parade. Street parking is available on Bunnerong Road and the surrounding side streets.

By bus: the 390X, 391 and 393 services stop on Bunnerong Road a short walk from the clinic.

Access: the clinic is a ground-floor shopfront with step-free entry.

Payments, private health and Medicare

We accept EFTPOS, credit card and cash. HICAPS is available on site, so if your private health extras cover includes chiropractic you can claim on the spot and pay only the gap. We also accept Medicare Chronic Condition Management Plan referrals (previously known as EPC or CDM plans) from your GP.

Fees are confirmed when you book and are listed on our online booking page.
