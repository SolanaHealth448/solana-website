Meet the team at Solana Health

Good care should be close to home and built on real relationships. Solana Health is led by chiropractor Angelo Marketos, with more practitioners joining as the clinic grows.

Dr Angelo Marketos (Chiropractor)

Principal Chiropractor

Angelo has been practising as a chiropractor since 2011. He holds a Bachelor of Chiropractic Science and a Master of Chiropractic, completed alongside research into the relationship between neck pain and balance. He is registered with the Chiropractic Board of Australia (AHPRA registration CHI0001597312) and is a member of Chiropractic Australia.

His approach is hands-on and conservative, focused on relieving pain and restoring movement through spinal adjustments, mobilisation, soft tissue work and dry needling. Angelo has particular experience helping desk workers, students and tradespeople manage and recover from pain, and takes the time to understand each patient's history and goals before building a plan together.

Angelo lives locally and opened Solana Health with his wife Jess to bring evidence-informed allied health care to the Matraville community.

Areas of practice

  • Neck pain
  • Headaches
  • Lower back pain
  • Sciatica
  • Shoulder injuries
  • Rib and chest wall complaints
  • Knee and ankle sprains and strains
  • Sporting injuries
  • Dry needling
